# Football mocap clips

Source: **Mocap Pack 05: Soccer** by Anderson Rohr
https://andersonrohr.gumroad.com/l/mocap_pack5

The pack is distributed as a free download and the author permits use in games, cinematics and VFX. The raw FBX pack is kept outside the runtime assets in `mocap_source/`; only the converted, retarget-ready clips in `assets/football/clips/` are shipped. Keep this attribution with the project.
